# Code of Conduct

White Label welcomes constructive participation from people with different backgrounds and experience levels. Project spaces should remain professional, technical, and useful.

Be respectful, give actionable feedback, accept correction, and keep disagreements focused on code, documentation, design, and project outcomes.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory or demeaning comments, publication of private information, sexualized conduct, spam, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through an established GitHub contact channel. Maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation when necessary to protect the project community.
