# Code of Conduct

White Label welcomes constructive participation from people with different backgrounds and experience levels. Project spaces should remain professional, technical, and useful.

Expected behavior includes respectful discussion, actionable feedback, evidence-based disagreement, accepting correction, and keeping criticism focused on code, documentation, design, and project outcomes.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory or demeaning comments, deliberate intimidation, publication of private information, sexualized conduct, spam, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through an established GitHub contact channel. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the project community.
